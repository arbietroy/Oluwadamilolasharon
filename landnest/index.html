How we build

Six stages. One team.
Photos at every one.

You get a site report with photos every Friday, from the first survey to handover.

01

Title & survey

We verify the land at the state Lands Registry, chart the survey plan and confirm there is no government acquisition on it before you pay a kobo.

02

Soil test & foundation

A geotechnical test decides the footing. Then we pour the reinforced slab and the damp-proof course, and let it cure properly.

03

Blockwork to lintel

Our masons lay the walls to drawing, with a site engineer checking plumb and level every day.

04

Doors, windows & MEP

Openings are framed, conduits and plumbing go in before plaster, and every circuit is tested.

05

Roofing

Treated timber trusses and stone-coated steel roofing with a 30-year manufacturer warranty.

06

Finishing & keys

Render, paint, tiles, kitchen and landscaping. You walk the snag list with us, then take the keys and your C of O.
