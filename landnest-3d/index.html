Questions

Before you visit

Is the land already titled?

Yes. Every plot has a Certificate of Occupancy before it goes on sale. We show you the survey plan and the Lands Registry search at your site visit.

Can I change the layout?

Room sizes and finishes can change before the slab is poured. Our architect redraws the plan and the price is fixed again in writing.

What if I live abroad?

Most of our buyers do. You get a site report with photos every Friday and a video call at each payment stage.

What does the warranty cover?

Twelve months on everything we build, and ten years on the structure. The roof carries the manufacturer's thirty-year warranty.
